Quality documents for botanical ingredients

Understand which technical documents to request before approving a botanical ingredient for commercial use.

Technical data sheet

Request the product identity, plant part, grade, physical specifications, storage and packaging details.

Certificate of analysis

Request a batch-specific report where required; agree on relevant microbial, chemical and residue testing limits.

Certificates and scope

Confirm current documents, issuer, validity, product and facility scope directly with Verdleaf. This preview does not assert active certification.
