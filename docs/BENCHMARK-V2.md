## Change decisions
- Dual visitor paths above fold, shared canonical catalogue and navigation.
- Botanical Atlas + product transformations; original illustration and macro photography; not generic template.
- Comparison moved from expansion to launch; public passport and Finder launch; combined quote/sample/document request, shortlist, print/share and custom sourcing launch.
- Seasonal editorial content and guided still/video tour launch, full 360 later.
- Responsible sourcing evidence and approved case studies, actual team contacts, buying guide, SEO health dashboard and event QR attribution.
- Private batch records/portal deferred pending operational data and access controls. No retail checkout, compulsory signup, fake live counters, auto-generated articles, heavy WebGL or sound.
- Previous schedule superseded; no quantified uplift or perfect score guaranteed. Prototype and acceptance gates define readiness.
- V1 remains recoverable in main history. V2 integrated into README and four core plans; if older passages differ, current V2 priority and gating sections take precedence.

## Verification gaps
Exact hosting, actual photos and rights, complete catalogue export, certified scope and dates, production capabilities, verified agricultural dates, search baseline, real buyer testing, native Arabic review, and browser/device visual review remain open. Document gaps before development rather than invent values.
