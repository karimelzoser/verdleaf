## Product data dictionary
Required public: title EN/AR, approved botanical name/plant part, slug, category, original descriptions, country/region, forms/grade, approved photos, uses, RFQ link. Optional when validated: planting/growing/harvest months by region/year, processing, physical appearance, particle size, moisture/purity/foreign matter, pesticide/heavy metal/microbiology parameters with units/method/limits, packing materials/net weights, storage/shelf life, MOQ, lead time, delivery terms, TDS, public sample COA, applicable certificates. Model variant SKU and grade separately, use explicit units and validation. Lot-specific documents private unless approved. Unknown fields remain null; public UI omits them or says “Ask our export team.” No invented availability, pricing or technical specs. A crop date is not inventory. Record source, reviewer, approval timestamp, version; bilingual independent approval. Import CSV with dry-run, duplicate detection, report and rollback.

## Other content records
Certificates: official name, issuer, number, scope, covered products/facility, issue/expiry dates, approved PDF, verification URL, publication status; reminders 90/60/30 days. Existing displayed marks EU Organic, USDA/NOP, JAS, COR and FDA-related registration need original documents and verification; FDA registration is not product approval. Additional ISO/HACCP/FSSC only if evidenced.
Events: name, official URL, location, dates, booth, participation type, approved media, meeting request. Visits: date, purpose, consent, approved story/media, optional anonymization. Farms: verified area/unit/ownership (prior site inconsistent acres vs feddans), regions and crops. Facilities: owned vs contracted, verified services and capacity. Markets: confirmed shipments vs intended markets; evidence and privacy. Company: distinguish heritage since 1995 from current legal incorporation; verify public contacts and brand identity. 
Media: metadata, image renditions, accessibility, rights, source and approval. Article: author/reviewer, date, topic, sources, links, revision.

## SEO implementation
Map commercial intent to one canonical page. EN root, /ar/ equivalent. SSR semantic HTML, visible main content, crawlable anchors, unique titles/descriptions/H1, clean slugs, canonical, reciprocal hreflang + x-default, XML sitemaps, robots, image alt, breadcrumb, social metadata. Draft/staging private or noindex; prevent indexing admin/RFQ/cart/search and unapproved pages; avoid filter URL explosion; maintain accessible crawlable category pagination. Implement Organization, BreadcrumbList, Product/ProductGroup only where accurate; no fake price, review, offer or availability. Validate structured data. Preserve old URLs via measured one-to-one 301 mapping; test chains and 404s. Keep legacy content inventory/export, GSC baseline, redirect map, staging and rollback. GSC submit sitemap and track indexed pages. GA4 with appropriate consent; never send personal details in analytics. Link products to crop/industry/related guides and certificates.
