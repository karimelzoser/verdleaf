Bulk botanical ingredient sourcing guide

Prepare a useful quotation or sample enquiry by specifying ingredient identity, form, quantity, destination and technical requirements.

Define the ingredient

Use the commercial and botanical reference names. Confirm the plant part, form and desired grade before ordering.

Provide commercial requirements

Include quantity, packing preference, destination port and proposed delivery terms. Ask for lead time and availability rather than assuming stock.

Review the sample and documents

Ask for specifications and relevant test reports, then compare the sample with the agreed requirements.
