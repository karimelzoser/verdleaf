What to specify for a bulk enquiry

Flower or calyx integrity, cut size, foreign matter and colour. Include quantity, pack weight, destination, intended application and applicable testing requirements. Ask for the current technical data sheet before approval.

Crop planning and documentation

Ask for the origin, crop year and availability; harvest dates do not guarantee stock. Request documents appropriate to the ingredient and destination market. Sourcing guide · Quality documents.
